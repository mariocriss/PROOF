Built for people who train

PROOF connects the people around physical performance — without claiming features that are not in the current Android launch.

Athletes

Create an identity, track skills, add proofs, and follow your proof stack and timeline as your record grows.

Coaches

Review verification requests from athletes at shared gyms and attest to performances you have supervised.

Gyms

Manage memberships for athletes and coaches, approve requests, and keep your facility’s roster organized in one place.

Physical Passport

Your in-app Physical Passport summarizes confidence, skills, and proofs. You can export a PDF snapshot from the Android app. A public web passport page is not available in this launch.

Coach verification

When an athlete and coach share an approved gym membership, athletes can request coach verification on proofs. Approved verifications carry more weight in stack confidence.

Trust & safety

Users can block and report accounts. Account deletion is available in the app after password confirmation.

Current launch status

Android closed testing. The mobile app is preparing for Google Play closed testing. App download from this site is coming soon.

Not in this launch: in-app messaging, public web passport profiles, Health Connect, Garmin import, or cloud photo/file Storage uploads.
