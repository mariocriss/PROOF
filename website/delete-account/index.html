Delete your PROOF account

Google Play account-deletion information for the PROOF Android app.

Account deletion is performed inside the PROOF Android app. This page explains the process for store reviewers and users. There is no separate web login for deletion in this launch.

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open PROOF and sign in with the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Account (from More / settings paths in the app).
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.
  4. Enter your password when asked (re-authentication).
  5. Wait for deletion to finish. On success you return to the signed-out registration screen.

If Firestore cleanup fails, your sign-in remains active, you see a friendly error, and you can retry. The app does not delete Firebase Authentication until required data cleanup succeeds.

What is deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account (after cleanup succeeds).
  • Your user profile, identity, skills, proofs, timeline, public profile, and coach profile (if any).
  • Your personal and coach handle reservations (handles can be reused).
  • Friendships, friend requests, blocks, and coach relationship documents you participate in.
  • Verification requests where you are the athlete or the coach.
  • Reports you submitted about others.
  • Your gym memberships. If you created a gym, that gym is closed (memberships, gym handle, and gym document removed) so it is not left without an owner.

What may be retained

Moderation reports about you

Reports other users filed about your account may be retained for trust and safety. Your public handle on those reports is replaced with a deleted marker ([deleted]). An internal opaque user id may remain so reports can be correlated. Email, display name, avatar, and profile pages are not kept on the report. Retention target: up to 24 months or until an operator closes the report, whichever comes first.

Historical coach verification on other athletes’ proofs

If you were a coach, other athletes’ proofs are not deleted. Live coach account links are cleared and the proof can show that it was verified by a coach whose account is no longer available. The historical verification status is preserved.

Timing

Deletion typically completes within seconds for normal accounts. Larger histories may take longer. Completion means you are signed out and cannot sign in with that account.
