Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 26, 2026
Operator: Mario Zderic
Jurisdiction: The Netherlands

PROOF (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is a Physical Identity Platform that helps athletes, coaches, and gyms document skills and proofs, manage relationships, and build trust through coach verification. This Privacy Policy explains what information we process in the current launch build.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the PROOF mobile application and this website (including Privacy, Terms, Delete Account, and Support pages).

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use PROOF, we may process:

  • Account information: email address and authentication credentials managed by Firebase Authentication (email/password).
  • Profile and identity data: display name, handle, role, optional free-text location, bio, privacy/discoverability settings, skills, proofs, and timeline events you create.
  • Relationship data: friend requests and friendships, blocks, coach relationships, gym memberships, and verification requests between athletes and coaches.
  • Gym data: gym profiles you create or manage and membership records associated with those gyms.
  • Trust and safety data: reports you submit about other users (reason, optional details, and identifiers needed for review).
  • Diagnostics: crash and stability reports via Firebase Crashlytics in release builds.

3. Information we do not collect in this launch

  • We do not operate in-app messaging or chat.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs or sell personal information for advertising.
  • We do not use Google Analytics in this launch build.
  • We do not collect precise device location via GPS permissions. Any “location” on a profile is optional text you type.
  • We do not use Health Connect.
  • We do not use Firebase Storage for photo or file uploads in this launch (avatar, proof media, and gym logo uploads are disabled).
  • We do not provide a public web passport profile page in this launch.

4. How we use information

  • Create and maintain your account and signed-in sessions.
  • Display profiles, skills, proofs, timelines, and passport summaries inside the app.
  • Enable friendships, blocking, coach relationships, gym memberships, and coach verification.
  • Review abuse reports and keep the service secure and stable.
  • Operate infrastructure via Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Crashlytics).

5. Sharing and visibility

Other signed-in users may see profile details according to your privacy settings, friendships, and coach or gym relationships. We do not sell your personal information. Service providers such as Google Firebase process data on our behalf to host authentication, database, and crash reporting.

6. Reports and moderation

If you report another user, we store the report reason, optional details you provide, your account identifier, and an internal identifier for the reported account. If the reported account is later deleted, we remove their public username from the report and replace it with a deleted-account marker. We may keep the internal identifier and report contents for up to 24 months to investigate abuse and protect the community. We do not keep the deleted user’s email, display name, avatar, or profile page in the report record.

7. Data retention and deletion

We retain your data while your account is active. You can delete your account in the Android app (Account → Delete account) after confirming your password. Deletion removes your Firebase Authentication user and associated personal Firestore data after cleanup succeeds. Limited moderation records and historical coach-verification markers on other athletes’ proofs may be retained as described on our Delete Account page. Cloud providers may retain backups for a limited period according to their infrastructure policies.

8. Children

PROOF is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes
